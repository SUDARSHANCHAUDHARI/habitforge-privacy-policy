## 🎯 About HabitForge

**HabitForge** is a gamified habit tracker Android app with RPG-style leveling, streaks, and achievements. The app helps you:

* 🎯 **Habit Tracking**: Create and track daily, weekly, and custom habits
* 🏆 **RPG Elements**: Level up system with XP, streaks, and achievements
* 📊 **Statistics Dashboard**: View progress, streaks, and completion trends
* 🔔 **Smart Reminders**: Daily habit reminders with WorkManager
* 📸 **Photo Attachments**: Attach photos to habit completions
* 📍 **Location Reminders**: Location-based reminder infrastructure
* 📊 **Advanced Analytics**: Heatmap calendar and weekly trend charts
* 🎮 **Daily Quests**: Advanced gamification with daily quests
* 🤖 **Habit Insights**: AI-powered habit insights and suggestions
* 🔗 **Social Sharing**: Share achievements, progress, and level-ups

**Learn more**: [Main HabitForge Repository](https://github.com/SUDARSHANCHAUDHARI/HabitForge)

## 🔒 Privacy-First Design

HabitForge is built with **privacy as a core principle**. We believe your personal habits, progress, and achievements should remain private and secure.

### Core Privacy Principles

1. **Local-First Storage**: All your personal data is stored locally on your device
2. **No Cloud Storage**: We don't store your data on any remote servers
3. **No User Accounts**: No account creation required - reducing data exposure
4. **Minimal Data Collection**: We only collect what's necessary for app functionality
5. **Transparent Practices**: Clear communication about what data we access and why

---

## 📊 What Data We Collect

### Personal Content (Stored Locally Only)

* **Habits**: Your habit tracking data, including:  
   * Habit name, description, and category
   * Frequency (daily, weekly, custom)
   * Difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard)
   * Custom icons and colors
   * Reminder settings
   * Creation date and sort order
* **Habit Completions**: Your completion history, including:  
   * Completion dates and timestamps
   * XP earned per completion
   * Notes attached to completions
   * Photo attachments (stored locally)
   * Location data (if location reminders are enabled)
* **User Progress**: Your gamification data, including:  
   * Current level and XP
   * Achievement unlocks
   * Daily quests and completions
   * Streak information
   * Habit insights generated locally

### App Preferences (Stored Locally Only)

* Theme settings (Light, Dark, System Default, Dynamic Color)
* Notification preferences (enabled/disabled)
* Onboarding completion status
* User level and XP progress

### What We DON'T Collect

* ❌ Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
* ❌ Contact information
* ❌ Payment information (billing handled by Google Play)
* ❌ Biometric data
* ❌ Device identifiers for tracking
* ❌ Analytics data (unless explicitly enabled)

## 💾 Data Storage & Security

### Local Storage

**All your personal data is stored locally on your device using:**

* **Room Database**: Encrypted local database for habits, completions, achievements, and user progress
* **DataStore**: Secure local storage for app preferences
* **No Cloud Sync**: Your data never leaves your device

### Security Measures

* ✅ **Encryption**: All local data is encrypted using Android's built-in encryption
* ✅ **No Network Transmission**: Habits and completions are never sent over the network
* ✅ **Secure Storage**: Photos and sensitive data stored securely
* ✅ **Regular Updates**: Security patches and updates applied regularly

### Data Retention

* **Habits**: Retained until you delete them manually
* **Completions**: Retained until you delete them manually
* **Achievements**: Retained permanently for progress tracking
* **User Progress**: Retained for level and XP tracking
* **Photos**: Stored locally until you delete the completion
* **Uninstall**: All data is deleted when you uninstall the app

## 🔐 Permissions Explained

### Required Permissions

| Permission                   | Purpose                             | Why Needed                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **INTERNET**                 | Connect to Google Play Billing      | Optional - only for Pro version purchases                             |
| **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**       | Send push notifications             | Daily habit reminders based on your preferences                       |
| **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM**     | Precise notification scheduling     | Accurate reminder timing                                              |
| **USE_EXACT_ALARM**          | Exact alarm scheduling              | Reliable habit reminders                                              |

### Optional Permissions

| Permission                   | Purpose                             | Why Needed                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION**     | Location-based reminders             | Optional - only if you enable location reminders                     |
| **ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION**   | Approximate location for reminders  | Optional - only if you enable location reminders                     |
| **ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION**| Background location for reminders  | Optional - only if you enable location reminders                     |
| **CAMERA**                   | Take photos for habit completions   | Optional - only if you want to attach photos                         |
| **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE**    | Access photos from gallery           | Optional - only if you want to attach photos (Android 12 and below)  |
| **READ_MEDIA_IMAGES**        | Access photos from gallery           | Optional - only if you want to attach photos (Android 13+)           |

### Privacy Controls

* ✅ **Offline Mode**: App works without internet connection
* ✅ **Notification Control**: Enable/disable notifications anytime
* ✅ **Location Control**: Enable/disable location reminders anytime
* ✅ **Photo Control**: Choose whether to attach photos
* ✅ **Data Export**: Export your habits and completions anytime (JSON, CSV, PDF)
* ✅ **Data Deletion**: Delete individual habits, completions, or all data

## 👤 Your Rights

### Data Control

* **View Your Data**: Access all your habits, completions, and progress in the app
* **Edit Your Data**: Modify or delete habits and completions anytime
* **Export Your Data**: Export habits and completions to JSON, CSV, or PDF (Settings → Advanced Settings → Export Data)
* **Delete Your Data**: Clear individual items or all data (Settings → Advanced Settings → Delete All Data)
* **Uninstall**: Uninstalling removes all local data

### GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you're in the European Union, you have additional rights:

* ✅ **Right to Access**: View all your personal data
* ✅ **Right to Rectification**: Edit inaccurate data
* ✅ **Right to Erasure**: Delete your data at any time
* ✅ **Right to Data Portability**: Export your data in portable formats (JSON, CSV, PDF)
* ✅ **Right to Object**: Disable analytics (if implemented)

**How to Exercise**: Use the app's built-in features in Settings → Advanced Settings or contact us directly.
