## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

#### Habit Data
* Habit names, descriptions, and categories
* Frequency settings (daily, weekly, custom)
* Difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard)
* Custom icons and colors
* Reminder settings and times
* Location-based reminder settings (if enabled)

#### Completion Data
* Completion dates and timestamps
* XP earned per completion
* Notes attached to completions
* Photo attachments (stored locally on your device)
* Location data (only if location reminders are enabled)

#### User Progress Data
* Current user level and XP
* Achievement unlocks and progress
* Daily quests and completions
* Streak information
* Habit insights (generated locally)

#### App Preferences
* Theme settings (Light, Dark, System Default, Dynamic Color)
* Notification preferences
* Onboarding completion status

### 2.2 Information We DON'T Collect

We do NOT collect:
* Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
* Contact information
* Payment information (handled by Google Play)
* Biometric data
* Device identifiers for tracking purposes
* Analytics data (unless explicitly enabled by you)

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information

**None**. HabitForge does not automatically collect any information from your device. All data is created and stored locally by you.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Local Data Usage

All your data is used **locally on your device** for:

* **Habit Tracking**: Display and manage your habits
* **Progress Tracking**: Calculate XP, levels, streaks, and achievements
* **Reminders**: Send notifications based on your preferences
* **Analytics**: Generate local statistics and insights
* **Export**: Allow you to export your data in various formats

### 3.2 No Data Transmission

**We do NOT transmit your data to any servers.** All processing happens locally on your device.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Retention

### 4.1 Local Storage

All your data is stored **locally on your device** using:

* **Room Database**: Encrypted local database for:
  * Habits
  * Habit completions
  * User levels and XP
  * Achievements
  * Daily quests
  * Habit insights
* **DataStore**: Secure local storage for app preferences
* **File System**: Photos attached to completions (stored in app's private directory)

### 4.2 Data Retention

* **Habits**: Retained until you delete them manually
* **Completions**: Retained until you delete them manually
* **Achievements**: Retained permanently for progress tracking
* **User Progress**: Retained for level and XP tracking
* **Photos**: Stored locally until you delete the completion
* **App Preferences**: Retained until you change or reset them

### 4.3 Data Deletion

* **Manual Deletion**: You can delete individual habits, completions, or all data through the app's Settings → Advanced Settings
* **Uninstall**: Uninstalling the app removes all local data
* **Export Before Deletion**: You can export your data before deletion (Settings → Advanced Settings → Export Data)

## 6. Permissions & Why We Need Them

### 6.1 Required Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Why Needed |
|------------|---------|------------|
| **INTERNET** | Connect to Google Play Billing | Only for optional Pro version purchases |
| **POST_NOTIFICATIONS** | Send push notifications | Daily habit reminders based on your preferences |
| **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM** | Precise notification scheduling | Accurate reminder timing |
| **USE_EXACT_ALARM** | Exact alarm scheduling | Reliable habit reminders |

### 6.2 Optional Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Why Needed |
|------------|---------|------------|
| **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION** | Location-based reminders | Only if you enable location reminders |
| **ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION** | Approximate location | Only if you enable location reminders |
| **ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION** | Background location | Only if you enable location reminders |
| **CAMERA** | Take photos for completions | Only if you want to attach photos |
| **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE** | Access photos from gallery | Only if you want to attach photos (Android 12-) |
| **READ_MEDIA_IMAGES** | Access photos from gallery | Only if you want to attach photos (Android 13+) |

**You can revoke any optional permission at any time through your device settings.**

## 8. Your Rights & Controls

## 10. Data Export & Portability

### 10.1 Export Formats

You can export your data in three formats:

* **JSON**: Complete data structure for backup/restore
* **CSV**: Spreadsheet-compatible format for analysis
* **PDF**: Human-readable report with statistics

### 10.2 How to Export

1. Open HabitForge
2. Go to Settings → Advanced Settings
3. Tap "Export Data"
4. Choose format (JSON, CSV, or PDF)
5. File will be saved to your device's Downloads folder
